## Why Netflix Libraries Vary by Country

Netflix operates on a country-by-country basis. When a movie studio or TV network produces a show, they sell the distribution rights to different companies in different regions:
* A show might be owned by Warner Bros. in the US, but licensed to a local network in France.
* In some countries, Netflix buys the global rights, making it a "Netflix Original."
* In other cases, Netflix only owns the rights in the United States. 

Because of this, Netflix must check your physical location before displaying its catalog. It does this by reading your device's IP address. If your IP address shows you are outside the US, the exclusive US titles are filtered out.

A VPN solves this by routing your web connection through a server in the United States. Once connected, Netflix sees a US-based IP address and displays the full American catalog, no matter where you are actually sitting.

## How to Solve the Netflix Proxy Error

If you connect to a US server and see a screen saying *"You seem to be using an unblocker or proxy,"* don't worry. Try these quick troubleshooting steps:

* **Switch Servers:** Disconnect from your current US server and connect to a different city (e.g., switch from New York to Miami).
* **Clear Your Cookies:** Netflix stores cookies that remember your previous location. Clear your browser cache and cookies or try using an incognito window.
* **Disable WebRTC:** WebRTC can leak your real IP address. Turn it off in browser settings or use a browser extension.
