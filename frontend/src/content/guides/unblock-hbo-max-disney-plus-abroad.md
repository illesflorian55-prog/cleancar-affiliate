## Why Max and Disney+ Block Connections

Every time you connect to the internet, your device is assigned an IP (Internet Protocol) address. This IP address functions as a digital mailing address, indicating your general physical location to any website or app you visit.

When you launch Max or Disney+:
1. The platform checks your **IP address**.
2. If your IP indicates you are in a country where they do not have broadcasting rights for a particular show (or where the platform isn't launched yet), your access is restricted.
3. If you use a VPN, your web connection is routed through a secure server in your home country. The platform only sees the VPN server's IP address, bypassing the block.
