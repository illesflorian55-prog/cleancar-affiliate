## What is ISP Bandwidth Throttling?

Internet Service Providers (ISPs) often oversell their bandwidth. When 8 PM hits and everyone in your neighborhood logs on to stream Netflix or play *Call of Duty*, the ISP's infrastructure gets overloaded.

To prevent a total collapse, their Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) systems look for high-bandwidth activities—like multiplayer gaming and 4K streaming—and artificially slow down (throttle) those specific connections. 

## The DDoS Threat in Competitive Gaming

Beyond throttling, high-rank players face a much more malicious threat: **DDoS attacks**. Salty opponents can use cheap online tools to extract your IP address through P2P voice chats or game servers, flooding your router with junk data and disconnecting you from the match.
