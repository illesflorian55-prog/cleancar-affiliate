# About The VPN Shield

The VPN Shield was founded on a simple principle: **privacy is a fundamental human right, not a luxury.** 

In an era of rampant ISP data harvesting, aggressive government surveillance, and predatory public Wi-Fi hackers, finding a reliable VPN has never been more critical. However, the VPN industry is plagued by paid reviews, hidden logging practices, and exaggerated claims. We built The VPN Shield to cut through the noise.

Our team consists of former cybersecurity analysts, network engineers, and privacy advocates who rigorously test every VPN provider we feature. We don't accept sponsored rankings. If a VPN fails our leak tests or slows down our connection beyond acceptable limits, it gets immediately removed from our recommendations.

# Our Testing Methodology

To provide the highest level of E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness), we subject every VPN to a brutal 5-stage testing process.

## 1. The "Kill Switch & Leak" Test
A VPN is useless if it leaks your real IP address during a momentary connection drop. 
- **How we test:** We run automated scripts to forcefully sever the internet connection while connected to the VPN. We monitor network packets using Wireshark to ensure the Kill Switch activates instantly and absolutely zero DNS, IPv6, or WebRTC data leaks out.

## 2. Global Speed & Throttling Analysis
We do not rely on the speeds advertised on VPN homepages.
- **How we test:** We run daily speed tests using 1Gbps fiber connections from multiple global locations (US, UK, Japan, Australia). We test the latency (ping) for gaming and the sustained download speeds for P2P torrenting. Only providers offering high-speed protocols like **WireGuard®** or **NordLynx** pass our benchmark.

## 3. The "Great Firewall" & Geo-Block Bypass
Streaming services and authoritarian governments spend millions blocking VPNs.
- **How we test:** We have researchers located in highly restrictive environments (including college campuses and heavily censored countries). We test if the VPN can consistently bypass deep packet inspection (DPI) to access blocked content like US Netflix, BBC iPlayer, or restricted gaming servers without detection.

## 4. Real-World Privacy Policy Audits
"No-logs" is a marketing buzzword. We demand proof.
- **How we test:** We pore over the Terms of Service and Privacy Policies. We only recommend VPNs that operate strictly on **RAM-only servers** (which wipe all data upon reboot) and have undergone **independent third-party security audits** (such as audits by PwC or Deloitte).

## 5. Value and Customer Support
Even the best software occasionally fails.
- **How we test:** We submit complex technical queries to the VPN's 24/7 live chat at 3:00 AM on a Sunday. We measure response times and evaluate whether the agent provides a genuine solution or just a generic copy-pasted script. We also verify that their 30-day money-back guarantee is actually honored without hassle.
